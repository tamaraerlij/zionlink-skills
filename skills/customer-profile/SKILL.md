# Step 1: Best Fit Customer Profile

Read `zionlink-market-entry.md` in the current folder. If it is missing, run the start skill first. Use simple English, short sentences, one question at a time, and no dashes in prose (dates, file names, list bullets and web addresses are fine).

## Rules
- Everything here is a **hypothesis, not verified**. Say it at the top of the result.
- Do not invent companies, numbers or statistics. Describe types only.
- Nothing is sent to anyone.

## What to do
1. Using the saved basics, ask at most 4 short questions to sharpen the picture. Pick only the ones that are still unknown:
   - What problem does the customer have before they buy from you?
   - What happens if they do nothing?
   - Who in the company feels the problem, and who signs?
   - What was your best sale so far, in one sentence?
2. Write the profile in this shape. Keep each part to 1 to 3 short lines:
   - **Best fit customer:** the company type, size and situation.
   - **Why they would buy:** the problem and the trigger that makes it urgent.
   - **Who decides:** the buyer, the user and who can block it.
   - **Rule out if:** two or three signs that this is a bad fit.
   - **What we still do not know:** the main open questions to test.
   - **How to test it:** 3 questions to ask in the first 3 conversations.
3. Show the profile and ask: "Does this sound like your best customer? What should change?" Edit until the person approves.
4. Save the approved profile to `zionlink-market-entry.md` under a new heading and mark step 1 as approved with today's date.

## Close
Tell the person what you just made in one line, then offer the next step: `/zionlink-market-entry:priority-countries`.

# Step 3: Targets and partners

Read `zionlink-market-entry.md`. Steps 1 and 2 must be approved. If not, offer to do them first. Use simple English, short sentences, one question at a time, and no dashes in prose (dates, file names, list bullets and web addresses are fine).

## Rules
- You do **not** have a verified company list. Never invent a company, a person, a phone number or an email. Give **types** of targets and ways to find real ones.
- Everything here is a **hypothesis, not verified**. Say this at the top.
- Nothing is sent to anyone. Do not open accounts, scrape websites or contact anyone.

## What to do
1. Use the approved customer profile and the first country. Ask only what you still need:
   - Which route do you prefer first: direct buyers, a local partner or a distributor?
   - Is there any type of company you already know you do not want?
2. Write three short lists:
   - **Target types:** 4 to 6 types of company that fit the profile, each with one line on why and who to look for inside it.
   - **Partner types:** 3 or 4 types of local partner, distributor or advisor, each with one line on what they would do for you.
   - **Where to find real ones:** for each type, 2 or 3 places or search phrases in the local language and in English (industry associations, trade fairs, public registries, professional networks, directories). Mark which ones cost nothing to use.
3. Add a **fit check**: 5 questions to ask about any real company before you spend time on it. Include one for signs the company is real and legal to deal with (for example a public registry entry, a working website, no sanctions). Say that checking this is the person's job, or ZionLink's platform can do it.
4. Show the result and ask: "Which types look most useful? What should change?" Edit until approved.
5. Save the approved lists to `zionlink-market-entry.md` and mark step 3 approved with today's date.

Say in one line what was decided, then offer the next step: `/zionlink-market-entry:first-approach`.

# Step 2: Priority countries

Read `zionlink-market-entry.md`. Step 1 must be approved. If not, offer to do it first. Use simple English, short sentences, one question at a time, and no dashes in prose (dates, file names, list bullets and web addresses are fine).

## Rules
- Everything here is a **hypothesis, not verified**. You work from general knowledge, not live data. Say this at the top.
- Do not invent market sizes, growth rates, prices or statistics. Use words like "likely", "often", "check this".
- Nothing is sent to anyone.

## What to do
1. If the person has markets in mind, use them (2 to 4 countries). If not, propose 3 candidates and say why each one is a candidate. Ask: "Do you want to change this list?"
2. Ask at most 3 questions that change the ranking, only if still unknown:
   - Do you have any customer, partner or contact in a country already?
   - Are there rules, certificates or languages that could stop you?
   - How fast do you need a first result?
3. For each country write a short card:
   - **Why it could work** (hypothesis)
   - **Main risk** (hypothesis)
   - **How buyers usually buy** (direct, partner, distributor) (hypothesis)
   - **What to check first** (one concrete check, for example a rule, a certificate, a competitor)
   Put the word "hypothesis" on every claim about a country, because you are using general knowledge, not live data.
4. Give a clear recommendation: one first country, one second, and one to wait. Say what would change your mind.
5. Show the result and ask: "Do you agree with the first country? What would you change?" Edit until approved.
6. Save the answers from question 2 (contacts, limits, speed) and the approved ranking to `zionlink-market-entry.md`, and mark step 2 approved with today's date.

## Close
Say in one line what was decided, then offer the next step: `/zionlink-market-entry:targets-and-partners`.
